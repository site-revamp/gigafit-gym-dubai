# GIGAFIT Dubai – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

GIGAFIT Dubai is a premium fitness and wellness club located in Al Quoz, Dubai. It serves fitness enthusiasts, strength-training members, group exercise participants and customers seeking professional coaching and wellness facilities.

The redesigned homepage presents GIGAFIT as a premium fitness destination, with a clearer focus on its training environments, facilities, memberships and visitor journey.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Clearer Homepage Messaging and Customer Journey
- Strengthened the hero section with a more direct brand message.
- Added clear pathways for visitors to **Plan Your Visit** and **Explore Membership**.
- Reduced competition between promotional content and the primary brand message.

**Addresses:** Unclear positioning and difficulty identifying the next step.

### 2. Better Facility and Class Presentation
- Organised the facility information into six clearly identified training categories.
- Improved the presentation of fitness activities and available training environments.
- Addressed unfinished schedule content, including repeated "Add a Title" placeholders, through a clearer class information structure.

**Addresses:** Poor facility discovery and unfinished class information.

### 3. More Direct Membership and Enquiry Pathways
- Made membership and visit actions more prominent and consistent.
- Improved access to contact information and enquiry options.
- Added persistent mobile actions for **Call the Club** and **Plan Your Visit**.

**Addresses:** Fragmented membership exploration and contact journeys.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual hierarchy:** Improved the prominence of the main message, key sections and conversion actions.
- **Content organisation:** Created a clearer structure for facilities, training categories and membership information.
- **User experience:** Made it easier for visitors to explore the club and identify relevant next steps.
- **Consistency:** Applied a more unified presentation to calls to action and information sections.
- **Responsive experience:** Prioritised accessible visit and contact actions for mobile visitors.
